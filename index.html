The Great Skill Circuit

Warm greetings from SparkNext Learning Hub. The Great Skills Circuit is a half-day immersive experience designed to spark creativity, curiosity, and confidence.

Built by our student executive interns, this event is designed for students and centered on collaboration, hands-on learning, and self-discovery.

In one half-day, you rotate through five skill stations, spending 45 minutes at each. Every stop is built to unlock a new skill, perspective, or hidden strength.

Our promise: 45 minutes | One new skill unlocked.

The 5 Skill Stations

"The Mystery Lab"
Escape-room style teamwork challenge focused on clues, logic, and speed.
"The Writing Den"
Creative writing space for short stories and expressive poetry.
"The Byte Zone"
Fun coding and digital challenges that combine logic and creativity.
"The Innovation Garage"
Team-based ideation where students design practical solutions to real problems.
"The Creative Studio"
Reel-making and graphite sketching to turn ideas into visual expression.

What to bring

  • A charged smartphone with internet access
  • Your laptop (if available)
  • An open, curious mindset ready to explore and learn
